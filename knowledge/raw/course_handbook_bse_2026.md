## 1. Programme Overview

The BSc Software Engineering is a four-year, full-time undergraduate programme of the Department of Computer Science in the Faculty of Engineering. The programme is accredited by the National Council for Higher Education.

The programme requires 480 credit units in total, distributed as 120 credit units per year of study. A full-time student takes between 15 and 24 credit units per semester, subject to the load limits in the Academic Handbook.

## 2. Programme Structure

| Year | Focus | Core credit units | Elective credit units |

| Year 2 | Data structures, databases, software design, networks | 105 | 15 |

## 4. Assessment Rules Specific to the Programme

Coursework in this programme consists of continuous assessment tests, practical assignments and group projects. Where a course has a group component, the group mark is moderated by an individual contribution factor derived from the version control history and the peer assessment return.

A student who does not submit a peer assessment return by the published date receives an individual contribution factor of 1.0 and forfeits the right to query the moderation.

The pass mark for every course is 50, as set by the Academic Handbook. There is no compensation between the coursework and examination components: a student who scores below 40 in the examination component is graded F for the course irrespective of the coursework mark.

## 5. Late Submission of Coursework

Coursework is submitted through the Learning Management System by the time and date stated in the assessment brief.

A submission that is late by up to twenty-four hours is marked and the mark is then reduced by ten percentage points. A submission that is late by more than twenty-four hours and up to seventy-two hours is capped at the pass mark of 50. A submission that is late by more than seventy-two hours is not marked and is graded zero.

These deductions are not applied where an Exceptional Circumstances application under the Academic Handbook has been upheld.

Technical failure of a student's personal equipment or personal internet connection is not accepted as grounds for an extension. Students are advised to submit a draft version well before the deadline, as the Learning Management System retains all submissions and marks the most recent one.

## 6. Industrial Training

Every student undertakes eight weeks of supervised industrial training in the recess term following Year 3. The placement is sought by the student and approved in advance by the Industrial Training Coordinator.

Assessment consists of the host supervisor's confidential report, a written training report of not more than five thousand words, and an oral presentation held in the second week of Semester I of Year 4. Industrial training carries 6 credit units and is graded pass or fail.

A student who fails industrial training repeats the placement in the following recess term and may not proceed to the Capstone Project until it is passed.

## 7. Capstone Project

The Capstone Project runs across both semesters of Year 4 and carries 12 credit units. Students work in groups of four to six members on a problem approved by the Department.

The assessment components are the project proposal, three progress demonstrations, the final software artefact and its repository, the group engineering report, and an individual viva voce.

Individual accountability is assessed through the version control history, the issue tracker and the individual viva. A student whose individual contribution cannot be evidenced in the repository may be awarded an individual mark that differs materially from the group mark.

## 8. Academic Advising

Every student is assigned a Personal Academic Tutor on first registration and retains the same tutor for the duration of the programme where possible.

The tutor meets the student at least once per semester, advises on course selection and progression, and is the first point of contact for academic difficulty. A student may request a change of tutor by writing to the Head of Department, and no reason need be given.
