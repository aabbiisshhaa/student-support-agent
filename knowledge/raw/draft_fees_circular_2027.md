## 1. Status

This is an internal working draft prepared for the Finance Committee. It has not been considered by Senate and does not represent University policy. It must not be quoted to students.

## 2. Proposed Changes

It is proposed that functional fees rise to UGX 540,000 per semester with effect from 2026/2027, and that the first instalment threshold rise from sixty per cent to seventy per cent of the semester liability.

It is further proposed that a hardship waiver of up to fifty per cent of functional fees be introduced for students in their third year and above who transfer from another accredited institution.

## 3. Next Steps

The draft is to be revised following the Finance Committee meeting and submitted to Senate.
