
## 1. Membership


Registration as a student of the University confers membership of the University Library. The University Identity Card serves as the library card. Membership is suspended automatically where a student is unregistered for a semester.

## 3. Borrowing


| Category | Items | Loan period | Renewals | |---|---|---|---| | Undergraduate | 4 | 14 days | 2 | | Postgraduate | 8 | 28 days | 3 | | Short Loan Collection | 1 | 3 hours, in-library | None | Renewal is performed on the Library catalogue or at the circulation desk, and is not permitted where another user has placed a hold on the item. An overdue item attracts a fine of UGX 1,000 per item per day for general collection items and UGX 2,000 per hour for Short Loan items. Borrowing is suspended where accumulated fines exceed UGX 20,000. A lost item is charged at the replacement cost of the item plus a processing charge of UGX 30,000. A student who later returns the item within ninety days is refunded the replacement cost but not the processing charge. Library fines and charges must be cleared before a student is issued a clearance certificate for graduation.


## 4. Electronic Resources


The Library subscribes to electronic databases covering engineering, computing, business and health. Access from within the campus network is automatic. Access from outside the campus is through the EZproxy link on the Library website, authenticated with the University email address and the Portal password. Electronic resources are licensed for individual academic use. Systematic downloading of a database or of a complete journal issue breaches the licence, and the publisher suspends access for the entire University when it is detected.


## 5. Study Spaces


The Library provides silent study areas on the third floor, group discussion rooms on the second floor, and an open reading area on the ground floor. Group discussion rooms are booked through the Library catalogue, for a maximum of three hours per group per day, up to seven days in advance. A room not occupied within fifteen minutes of the booked start time is released. Reserving a seat by leaving belongings unattended is not permitted. Items left unattended for more than thirty minutes are removed to the lost property desk.


## 6. Past Examination Papers


Past examination papers for the preceding five academic years are available in the Past Papers section of the Library website, organised by faculty, programme and course code. Marking schemes and model answers are not published and are not released on request.


## 7. Support for Research and Referencing


Subject librarians hold consultation hours during teaching weeks and support literature searching, database selection and referencing. Consultations are booked on the Library website. The University's required citation style for undergraduate work is set by the Faculty. The Library provides guides for APA 7th edition, IEEE and Harvard styles, and supports Zotero and Mendeley.


## 8. Conduct in the Library


Food is not permitted in the Library. Covered drinking containers are permitted except in the Special Collections room. Mobile telephones are set to silent throughout and calls are taken outside the building. Repeated breaches are reported to the Dean of Students under the Student Regulations.
