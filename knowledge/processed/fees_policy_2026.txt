
## 1. Scope


This Policy governs the assessment, payment and recovery of tuition and functional fees for all students of Nakawa University. It does not govern scholarships administered by external sponsors, which are governed by the agreement between the University and the sponsor.


## 2. Structure of Fees


A student is liable for two categories of charge in each semester:

- Tuition, assessed per credit unit at the rate published for the programme and year of entry. The rate applicable to a student is the rate in force at the student's first registration and is not varied during the normal programme duration.

- Functional fees, being a fixed amount per semester covering registration, examination, identity card, library, laboratory and medical services. Functional fees for 2025/2026 are UGX 480,000 per semester for all undergraduate programmes.


## 3. Payment and Instalments


Fees are payable by bank deposit or mobile money to the University collection accounts published on the Student Portal. Cash is not received at any University office. A student may pay in two instalments in each semester:

- A first instalment of not less than sixty per cent (60%) of the total semester liability, due on the date published in the Academic Calendar. This instalment must clear before the student can complete registration.

- The balance of forty per cent (40%), due on the balance date published in the Academic Calendar. A payment is recognised when it is reflected on the student's Portal account. A student who has deposited funds that are not reflected within three working days presents the deposit slip to the Finance Help Desk with their student number.


## 4. Consequences of Non-Payment


A student who has not cleared the first instalment by the registration deadline is not registered for the semester. A student who has not cleared the balance by the balance date has examination results for that semester withheld and displayed as WH on the Student Portal. Withheld results are released within three working days of the account being cleared. An outstanding balance from a previous semester bars registration in any subsequent semester and bars participation in graduation.


## 5. Refunds


A student who formally drops a course within the add/drop period has the tuition for that course credited in full to their Portal account. A student who withdraws from a course after the add/drop deadline, whether or not the withdrawal is approved, is not entitled to a refund or credit of tuition for that course. A student who defers a semester under the Academic Handbook has tuition already paid for that semester carried forward to the semester of return. Functional fees are not carried forward and are not refunded. A student who is discontinued or who leaves the University is refunded any credit balance on application, less the functional fees for the semester in progress. Applications for refund are made on Form FIN/4 and are paid within thirty working days.


## 6. Late Payment


A late payment surcharge of two per cent (2%) of the outstanding amount is applied for each calendar month or part month by which a payment is late. The surcharge is applied automatically by the Portal and is itself part of the outstanding balance.


## 7. Financial Hardship


A student who is unable to meet a published fees deadline may apply to the Bursar for a Revised Payment Plan. The application is made on Form FIN/9 before the deadline concerned and is supported by evidence of the circumstances. A Revised Payment Plan varies the dates on which amounts fall due. It does not reduce the total amount payable and it does not waive the late payment surcharge on amounts already overdue at the date of application. The Bursar decides an application within five working days. A student whose application is granted is treated as compliant with the deadline for the purposes of registration and examination eligibility, for as long as the Plan is honoured. A student who defaults on a Revised Payment Plan is immediately subject to Section 4.


## 8. Fees Statements


A student may generate a fees statement at any time from the Student Portal under Finance, then Statement of Account. The statement shows all assessments, payments, credits and surcharges for the current and all previous semesters. A stamped statement for presentation to a sponsor is obtained from the Finance Help Desk within two working days of request.
