
## 1. Status of this Calendar


This Calendar was approved by Senate on 18 June 2025. All dates are binding on students and staff. Where a date falls on a public holiday declared after approval, the affected activity moves to the next working day and the Academic Registrar publishes a notice on the Student Portal. All times stated are East Africa Time (UTC+3).

## 7. University Closure Days


The University is closed to teaching on gazetted public holidays of the Republic of Uganda. In addition, the University observes an administrative closure from 24 December 2025 to 2 January 2026 inclusive. Student Portal services remain available during the administrative closure, but the Academic Registry, Finance and Faculty offices are closed and no applications are processed.
