
## 1. Eligibility to Graduate


A student is eligible to graduate where all of the following are satisfied:

- The student has passed every course required by the programme and accumulated the total credit units required.

- The student has completed the programme within the maximum duration permitted by the Academic Handbook.

- The student has no outstanding tuition, functional fees, library charges or other University debt.

- The student has returned all University property and obtained clearance.

- The results have been confirmed by Senate.

A student who meets the academic requirements but has not obtained clearance is not presented for graduation and is deferred to the next ceremony.


## 2. Classification of Awards


The class of an undergraduate award is determined by the cumulative grade point average computed over all courses counting toward the award.

| Cumulative GPA | Class of award | |---|---| | 4.40 - 5.00 | First Class Honours | | 3.60 - 4.39 | Second Class Honours, Upper Division | | 2.80 - 3.59 | Second Class Honours, Lower Division | | 2.00 - 2.79 | Pass |

The cumulative grade point average is not rounded for the purposes of classification. A student with a cumulative grade point average of 4.39 is classified in the Upper Division and not in the First Class.

A student who has been found guilty of examination misconduct resulting in the nullification of a semester's results is not eligible for First Class Honours.


## 3. Clearance


Clearance is completed on the Portal under Graduation, then Clearance. The Portal collects confirmation from the Library, the Directorate of Finance, the Halls of Residence, the relevant Department and the Directorate of ICT Services.

Each unit confirms or raises an objection within five working days. An objection states the outstanding matter and the action required to resolve it. Clearance opens when the provisional graduation list is published and closes fourteen days before the ceremony.


## 4. The Provisional Graduation List


The provisional graduation list is published on the Portal on the date in the Academic Calendar. A student who expects to be on the list and is not, or whose name, programme or class is stated incorrectly, submits a query to the Academic Registrar before the published query deadline.

A query submitted after the deadline is considered for the following ceremony. Names are printed on certificates exactly as they appear on the admission record, and a change of name is effected only on presentation of a statutory instrument or other legal document before the query deadline.


## 5. The Ceremony


Graduands attend the ceremony in academic dress appropriate to the award. Academic dress is hired from the University at a charge published with the graduation notice, and is returned within three working days of the ceremony.

A graduand who does not wish to attend the ceremony indicates this on the Portal and graduates in absentia. Certificates for graduands in absentia are collected from the Academic Registry from ten working days after the ceremony, in person and on production of the University Identity Card, or by a representative holding a letter of authority and a copy of the graduand's identification.


## 6. Certificates and Transcripts


Each graduand is issued one certificate and one official transcript without charge.

A replacement certificate is issued only where the original is lost or destroyed, on a sworn declaration and payment of the published charge, and is marked as a replacement. Additional official transcripts are issued on request and on payment of the published charge, within five working days.

A verification of award for an employer or a further education institution is provided by the Academic Registry on written request from the institution concerned, with the graduand's consent.


## 7. Posthumous and Aegrotat Awards


Senate may confer an award posthumously where a student had substantially completed the programme.

Senate may confer an aegrotat award where a student is prevented by illness from completing the assessment and the available evidence indicates that the student would have passed. An aegrotat award is unclassified.
