
## 1. Halls of Residence


The University maintains five halls of residence with a combined capacity of one thousand four hundred beds. Halls are single-sex in their sleeping accommodation and shared in their common facilities.

A residence place is for one academic year and does not run through the recess term, except for students on approved recess-term academic activity, who apply separately.


## 2. Allocation


Applications for residence open on the Portal under Accommodation on the first working day of July and close on the last working day of July.

Where applications exceed capacity, places are allocated in the following order of priority:

- Students with a disability or medical condition requiring accommodation on campus, as evidenced by a Learning Support Agreement.

- First-year students whose home district is outside the Kampala metropolitan area.

- Finalist students.

- All other applicants, by ballot.

The outcome is published on the Portal within fourteen days of the closing date. An offer not accepted and paid for within seven days lapses and the place passes to the next applicant on the list.


## 3. Residence Charges


Residence charges are payable per semester, in advance, and are separate from tuition and functional fees. The charge for 2025/2026 is UGX 950,000 per semester for a shared room of four beds and UGX 1,400,000 per semester for a shared room of two beds.

A resident who vacates before the end of a semester forfeits the charge for that semester. A resident whose place is withdrawn for disciplinary reasons forfeits the charge for that semester.


## 4. Conditions of Residence


Every resident signs a Residence Agreement. The Agreement requires the resident to:

- Occupy only the allocated bed space and not to transfer or sublet it.

- Admit visitors only between 08:00 and 22:00, and to sign visitors in at the Hall desk. Overnight visitors are not permitted.

- Refrain from cooking in rooms. Cooking is permitted only in the Hall kitchens.

- Refrain from keeping electrical heating appliances, candles and other naked flames in rooms.

- Keep the room in a clean and safe condition and allow inspection on reasonable notice.

- Vacate the Hall within forty-eight hours of the end of the examination period.

Breach of the Residence Agreement is dealt with by the Warden and may be referred to the Dean of Students under the Student Regulations.


## 5. Maintenance and Repairs


A maintenance request is logged on the Portal under Accommodation, then Report a Fault, or at the Hall desk.

| Category | Examples | Target response | |---|---|---| | Emergency | Burst pipe, electrical hazard, failed external door lock | 4 hours | | Urgent | No water, no power to a room, blocked toilet | 24 hours | | Routine | Faulty socket, broken furniture, dripping tap | 5 working days |

A resident is charged for damage that is not fair wear and tear. Where responsibility cannot be attributed to an individual, the cost of damage to a shared area is shared among the residents of that area.


## 6. Security


Halls are staffed at the entrance at all hours. Residents present the University Identity Card on entry.

Rooms are fitted with individual locks. The University does not insure residents' personal property, and residents are advised to insure valuable items independently. Lost keys are reported immediately and are replaced at a charge of UGX 40,000.


## 7. Withdrawal of a Residence Place


A residence place is withdrawn where a student ceases to be registered, is suspended or expelled, defaults on residence charges for more than thirty days, or commits a serious breach of the Residence Agreement.

A student whose place is withdrawn for default may be readmitted to residence in a later semester on clearing the debt, subject to availability.


## 8. Private Accommodation


The University does not inspect, approve or endorse private accommodation, and is not a party to any agreement between a student and a private landlord.

The Dean of Students maintains a noticeboard of properties offered by landlords and a guidance note on matters to check before signing a tenancy. Students are advised not to pay any deposit before viewing a property and obtaining a written agreement.
